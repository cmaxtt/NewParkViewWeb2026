✦ 8 Treatable Clinical Conditions

Common Conditions We Assess & Prescribe For

If you are experiencing any of the following ailments, our certified pharmacists can assess your symptoms and provide prescription-grade treatment immediately.

Respiratory

Allergic Rhinitis & Seasonal Allergies

Sneezing, runny or itchy nose, persistent congestion, watery itchy eyes triggered by dust, pollen, or environmental irritants.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Non-drowsy 2nd Gen Antihistamines
  • Prescription Corticosteroid Nasal Sprays
  • Antihistamine Eye Drops
Consult Pharmacist
Viral / Dermatology

Cold Sores (Herpes Labialis)

Tingling, itching sensation followed by painful fluid-filled blisters on or around the lip margins that require rapid intervention.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Targeted Topical Antivirals (Acyclovir)
  • Anesthetic & Barrier Creams
  • Oral Antiviral Therapy (when indicated)
Consult Pharmacist
Dermatology

Dermatitis, Eczema & Pruritus

Dry, cracked, inflamed, intensely itchy skin patches or allergic contact reactions caused by soaps, chemicals, or weather changes.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Prescription Corticosteroid Ointments
  • Ceramide Barrier Creams
  • Oral Antipruritic Therapeutics
Consult Pharmacist
Gastrointestinal

Heartburn & Acid Reflux (GERD)

Burning retrosternal chest pain, regurgitation of stomach acid, and sour indigestion post-meal or when reclining.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Proton Pump Inhibitors (Omeprazole, Pantoprazole)
  • H2-Receptor Antagonists
  • Therapeutic Alginate Raft Suspensions
Consult Pharmacist
Skin & Allergy

Insect Bites, Stings & Hives

Painful, itchy, or swollen localized reactions from mosquitoes, sandflies, wasps, or sudden environmental urticaria hives.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Hydrocortisone Anti-Inflammatory Creams
  • Fast-acting Oral Antihistamines
  • Secondary Infection Antiseptics
Consult Pharmacist
Ophthalmic

Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)

Redness of the sclera, foreign body sensation, grittiness, eyelid crusting, and watery or mucoid discharge.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Antibacterial Eye Drops & Ointments
  • Sterile Lubricating Eye Washes
  • Ocular Decongestants & Compresses
Consult Pharmacist
Urological

Uncomplicated Urinary Tract Infections

Burning sensation upon urination, uncomfortable frequency, urgency, and pelvic discomfort without fever or flank pain.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Targeted Prescription Antimicrobial Regimens
  • Urinary Alkalinizers (Symptom Relief)
  • Prevention & Hydration Counseling
Consult Pharmacist
Musculoskeletal

Sprains, Strains & Joint Discomfort

Acute muscle pain, back strain, minor ligament sprains, or arthritic flare-ups resulting from physical work or exercise.

Pharmacist Treatments:

  • Topical NSAID Gels (Diclofenac)
  • Oral Anti-Inflammatory Analgesics
  • Therapeutic Supports & Cold/Heat Therapy
Consult Pharmacist
